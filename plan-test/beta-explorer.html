Model: CVR ~ Beta(1 + p·(n + w), 1 + (1−p)·(n + w)) — a Beta-Binomial posterior that assumes conversions come in at your guessed rate p. Cafés visited adds real-data weight; prior weight is how many visits your gut feeling is worth (0 = pure guess, flat start). The vertical density scale is relative and carries no meaning — only the position and width of the curve matter.
